Beyond the crown

More than a title. More than a stage.

Nicole Cordoves is a Filipino host, model, actress, and beauty queen. She first gained wider recognition after winning Miss Chinatown Philippines 2014, then represented the Philippines at Miss Grand International 2016, where she finished as first runner-up.

Her career has since expanded into live-event hosting, television, judging, acting, brand work, and pageant leadership. This site is an independent fan-made reference archive built from publicly available coverage; it is not an official website.
